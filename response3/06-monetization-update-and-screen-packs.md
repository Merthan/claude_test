# Monetization update: the catalogue with the coach, screen packs, passes, compliance

## Short answer

The catalogue from response 2 stands; the coach fills the Pro+ column that was waiting for a reason, the screens become small packs, and the event pass now covers everything for seven days because the thesis defence is where both the remote and the coach are wanted at once. Nothing is priced until April (with a stipend) or February (without); everything is badged from day one. Confidence: high on structure, medium on prices.

Three consumer products plus cosmetics remains the rule. The coach does not add a tier; it fills one.

## What is free in the coach, and why

The free coach must be good enough that a lecturer recommends it and a student uses it before every presentation, because that is where the standalone app's growth comes from and where the university story starts. Pace, pauses, pitch, volume, the clarity moments and the reel are the experience; they stay free. What a frequent user needs beyond that (history, rehearsal memory, speakers, exports, the deck) is Pro+. Fillers for English stay free because a count is the first thing people check and the thing they tell others about; limiting it would be remembered as stingy.

## Screen packs

| Pack | Contents | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Basics | black, white, blur, plain countdown with minutes only | free |
| Classroom kit | work timer with custom text and a chime that returns to the slides, "Q&A time" with the floating question marks, "Break: back at 10:45", "Quiet please", "Phones away" | Pro and up |
| Business kit | clean countdown, "We resume at", "Next up: speaker name", a neutral "Questions?" screen | Pro+ and Business |
| Fun kit | the cosmetic screens, seasonal items, the stunts | sold separately to everyone, including Pro |

Rules: at most three packs plus singles; the companion and the web viewer render the same screens; packs are purchased once and never expire; nothing in a pack is needed to present (so nobody is blocked mid-talk by a paywall); seasonal singles at €0.99 to €1.99 keep the Fun kit alive without new packs.

## Passes

The seven-day pass now covers Pro+ in full, because a person preparing for one event wants both the remote's extras and the coach's history for that week. €2.99 instead of €1.99 reflects that. Watch the share: if passes exceed 40% of revenue, the yearly plan is priced too close to them.

## Badging and the launch phase

Unchanged from response 2: every Pro and Pro+ feature carries its badge from day one with "free during the launch phase", the credit and the toast exist from day one, early users keep Pro, and the switch-on comes with new features rather than by taking something away. The coach's badges are the most visible ones, because people will see "Pro+" next to rehearsal memory the day they want it.

## Anchors

Speeko about $100 a year, Yoodli $96 to $240 a year, PowerPoint's Speaker Coach free with Microsoft 365, a cheap clicker €8 to €16, Logitech Spotlight €105 to €130. Pro+ at €24.99 a year sits at a quarter of the coaching apps and includes the remote; Pro+ Lifetime at €79 to €99 is four years of the yearly plan; the event pass is an impulse price.

## Compliance notes for the coach

- No medical or therapeutic claims, nothing about stuttering, anxiety or disorders; "practice" and "feedback" only.
- The consent notice and the "only my voice is kept" default (doc 01).
- Privacy labels: audio is processed on the device and not collected; the optional text sent for corrections is user content not linked to the user; say it that way and no other way.
- The microphone permission string names the purpose ("to analyse your rehearsal on this phone").
- Export compliance questions answered truthfully for the model decryption and request signing (doc 01).
- Offer codes for the "feedback after five talks, 50% off" banner (response 2), and a "founding user" code for the beta testers from the Toastmasters clubs.

## Expected mix after a year (a guess to be replaced by data)

Pro once 35%, Pro+ yearly 25%, Pro+ Lifetime 15%, event passes 10%, business 10%, cosmetics and packs 5%. If Pro+ in both forms is below 30% six months after pricing, the coach's paid features are not the ones people want, and the thumbs data will say which are.

## What I am not sure about

- **€24.99 versus €29.99 for Pro+ yearly.** The coach justifies more than the remote did; test after the launch phase.
- **Fillers free for English** could cost some Pro+ conversions; it buys recommendations, which I value more in year one.
- **Three packs** may still be one too many; if the Business kit sells nothing in six months, fold it into Pro+.
