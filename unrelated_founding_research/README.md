## Rules that shape the plan
- **Keep AnyPresent private and free** until decided. The consultation confirmed private app income does not count; a **company would**: Science & Startups requires "none of the team members may have founded a startup before", every stipend requires the funded venture not to be founded. Found a UG only when you consciously choose the company route (GründungsBONUS Plus) for that idea.
- **Exmatriculate when a stipend starts, not before.** EXIST pays the graduate rate (€2,500) only to non-enrolled graduates, and an enrolled solo applicant counts as a "student team" (normally not funded). BAföG ends with any of these stipends anyway (full-time rules, income offset).
- **One living-cost program at a time:** BSS, EXIST and Prototype Fund exclude each other while running. Applying to all is fine; accepting two is not.
- **Plan for tax, insurance and paperwork:** none of the stipends is reliably tax-free (BSS is paid gross, guides call EXIST taxable, Prototype Fund is a grant with unsettled income-tax status) — reserve 15–30 %. Leaving enrolment ends student health insurance (voluntary GKV ≈ €230–300/month). The Prototype Fund needs registered self-employment before the funding phase; registering Bluetooth Presenter as a Gewerbe *before* asking the funders can read as "founded before application" — ask first, file second. Details and the five written questions: `17-rules-bafoeg-taxes-legal.md`.
- **Open source is a one-way door:** the Prototype Fund only asks for it after selection, but once published it is published. Use it for ShadowAI's core (trust is a feature there), not for the speech engine.
