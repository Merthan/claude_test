## The verdict in five lines
1. **"No solo founders" is not true for Berlin as a whole.** It is TU's / Science & Startups' rule. The Startup Incubator Berlin (HWR) funds solo founders under the same Berliner Startup Stipendium (€2,500/month, 6–12 months), and EXIST allows solo founders by federal rule.
2. **Your best money-per-effort option is the Prototype Fund (deadline 30 Nov 2026):** €47,500 for 6 months as an individual (+€31,667 second stage), ~8 % odds, open source required. This is the "government money for open source that pays the bills" you described, and it starts 1 Jun 2027, right when BAföG runs out.
3. **EXIST is more realistic than TU made it sound**, but only if you exmatriculate: enrolled you would get €1,000 and count as a "student team", which is normally not funded; as a graduate you get €2,500 × 12 + €15k budgets. You need a professor as mentor and a host university — ask HTW/BHT/HWR/Potsdam if TU refuses.
4. **The one program that fits your ideal team** (a domain expert who explains the problem, no business co-founder) is **Vision Health Pioneers** (doctor tools; apply 15 Jan–28 Feb 2027; they help solo applicants find a clinician partner).
5. **The Gründungswettbewerb Digitale Innovationen is suspended** and the NLnet Commons Fund closed, so two classic solo-friendly routes are gone. Everything else in the lists is live.

## The grey zone around AnyPresent — decide early
- BSS hosts require "not yet incorporated"; Science & Startups adds "none of the team members may have founded a startup before"; Avant Now requires "no revenues yet"; EXIST requires the venture not to be founded. Prototype Fund requires open source.
- So: launching AnyPresent as a **paid app** under a company or contracts can make **AnyPresent itself** ineligible for BSS/EXIST at several hosts. It does **not** block applying with another idea (ShadowAI, Itemz, doctor tools, senior tool).
- Practical options: (a) launch AnyPresent free / as a beta until you know which stipend you pursue with it; (b) treat AnyPresent as the independent side business (like Bluetooth Presenter) and apply with a different idea; (c) go the company route for AnyPresent (UG + GründungsBONUS Plus + DBU-type grants) and keep stipends for a second idea. Question 6–7 in `00-questions-for-you.md`.
- Bluetooth Presenter (50k downloads, revenue) is your strongest evidence of execution. Disclose it everywhere; ask each host in writing whether it counts as "founded before".
