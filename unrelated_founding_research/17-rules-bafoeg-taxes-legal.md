# Practical rules: BAföG, tax, health insurance, "not yet founded", existing app income

Status 31 Oct 2026. Background for decisions, not legal or tax advice. Tags: [S] stated in a search excerpt of the cited page (page itself not opened); [M] background knowledge to mid-2026, unverified. Statutory figures are mostly [M] — confirm with the BAföG office, the Krankenkasse and one Steuerberater session.

## What this means for you (short)
1. **BAföG ends when a stipend starts.** The BSS FAQ (2022) lists BAföG among benefits not drawn in parallel [S]; EXIST excludes any other "Förderprogramm zur Finanzierung des Lebensunterhalts" [S]; neither fits the €300 scholarship exemption. At €2,500 the counted income exceeds the BAföG maximum anyway. Report the Bescheid at once (§ 60 SGB I); overpayments are reclaimed. Do **not** give up BAföG or de-register before a written Bewilligung exists.
2. **BAföG presumes real full-time study.** "Enrolled with zero modules" works only until the Amt asks (Leistungsnachweis after the 4th semester is far away for you, but mind the risk) [M]. Your plan to leave enrolment when a stipend starts is the safe one.
3. **EXIST tier:** a 1st-semester Master student cannot meet the student tier's "half of the studies completed at application" rule, and a ministry Q&A says an enrolled Master student counts as a student (→ €1,000) [S]. With the Bachelor you are an Absolvent (€2,500) once de-registered. Get PtJ's answer in writing on when status is fixed (application vs. start), then time the exmatriculation.
4. **Bluetooth Presenter income is not a grey zone for tax, only for funders.** Ongoing Play Store sales are normally a Gewerbe (registration, EÜR, BAföG disclosure) [M]. The TU consultation said it is too small to count for EXIST/BSS. **Registration trap:** registering it now creates a Gewerbeanmeldung date, which some programs read as "founded before application" [S, NBank analogy]. Ask the funder how a regularised older activity is treated **before** filing; a Steuerberater can advise on start date and late declaration.
5. **"Not yet founded" means:** EXIST — the company must not exist before funding starts, and revenue or a Gewerbe *in the context of the funded idea* is förderschädlich [S]. BSS — TU/Science & Startups: "not incorporated", "none of your team members have ever founded a startup" [S]; Humboldt: first-time founders unless no economic activity yet; BHT: not yet founded or not yet commercially active; IBB FAQ: a trade registered for a side job must be disclosed, participation "grundsätzlich möglich" [S].
6. **AnyPresent:** release it **free** (no IAP, ads, subscriptions, invoices) until the funding decision, or apply with a different idea. Monetising it under a Kleingewerbe would likely block EXIST/BSS *for AnyPresent* and is a judgement call per host even for another idea.
7. **Prototype Fund is not a stipend.** It is a project grant to a self-employed person (or GbR): freelancers need no Gewerbe, everyone else registers one before the funding phase; tax number or registration proof is requested; "echter Zuschuss" → no VAT; income-tax treatment unsettled ("Besorgt euch einen guten Steuerberater") [S]. Grant profit counts for BAföG and would end it.
8. **Do not plan on "tax-free".** BSS is paid gross ("2.500 Euro (brutto)") [S]; guides call the EXIST stipend taxable (Anlage SO; reserve 15–30 %) [S]; no source applied § 3 Nr. 44 EStG to EXIST or BSS. At €12,000/yr tax is ~zero (Grundfreibetrag €12,348 in 2026 [M]); at €30,000/yr it is not.
9. **Health insurance.** Student cover (KVdS) needs enrolment, age < 30 and ≤ 14 Fachsemester, and ends if the Kasse deems you "hauptberuflich selbständig" (§ 5 Abs. 5 SGB V) — a full-time founder stipend invites that question [M]. After de-registration: voluntary GKV roughly €230–300/month [M]. A Urlaubssemester usually keeps enrolment and often KVdS, but not BAföG. Ask your Kasse in writing.
10. **One living-cost program at a time:** BSS × EXIST excluded by both; Prototype Fund excluded while EXIST/BSS run. Applying to all is fine; accepting two is not.
11. **Numbers [M, verify]:** Kleinunternehmer (§ 19 UStG) €25,000 prior year / €100,000 current year; Gewerbesteuer allowance €24,500; BAföG allowance for own earnings tied to the Minijob limit (€603/month in 2026), applied to profit; BAföG asset limit under 30: €15,000 (saved grant money counts).
12. **Not available to you:** Gründungszuschuss (needs ALG I with ≥150 days left) and Einstiegsgeld (SGB II; students in BAföG-eligible studies excluded) [M]. Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs wants a business started < 3 years ago — an app sold since 2021 is probably outside the window unless unregistered [M]. TÜBİTAK BİGG / KOSGEB need a company in Turkey [M].

## Three clean paths for AnyPresent (synthesis)
- **A. Stipend for AnyPresent itself (EXIST/BSS):** free release, no revenue, written pre-check disclosing the old app; risks: same domain as Bluetooth Presenter, "ever founded" wording, EXIST's exclusion of "modifications of existing products without significant USP".
- **B. Stipend for a different idea (recommended):** AnyPresent stays a small side business; get the funder's consent in writing; keep its hours and income minimal during the full-time period.
- **C. Company route for AnyPresent:** UG + GründungsBONUS Plus (+ DBU-type grants); register self-employment properly; then EXIST/BSS are only possible for a second idea and may be affected by the "ever founded" rule at some hosts.

## Questions to put in writing
1. Science & Startups / host of choice: does an unregistered app earning ~€50–100/month since 2021 count as "founded" / "ever founded a startup"? Does a free AnyPresent release matter? Is a Urlaubssemester accepted?
2. PtJ (EXIST): Absolvent or Studierender for a Bachelor holder enrolled in a Master? Judged at application or start? Is BAföG excluded or counted? Side-work hour limit?
3. BAföG office (Studierendenwerk Berlin): how EXIST/BSS/Prototype Fund income is counted; reporting; consequences of few modules.
4. Krankenkasse: student cover during a full-time stipend, with and without Urlaubssemester; cost of voluntary insurance.
5. Steuerberater (one session): Gewerbe vs. Freiberufler for app income, late declaration and start date, stipend taxation, Prototype Fund treatment.
