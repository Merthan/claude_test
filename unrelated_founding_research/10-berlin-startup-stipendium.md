# Berliner Startup Stipendium (BSS) — detail file

Status date: 31 Oct 2026. Sources are search excerpts of the cited pages (the sandbox could not open the pages themselves), so quotes were not checked against full pages. Confidence: H = host's own page or several consistent sources; M = single source; L = third-party/old/contradicted.

## The short version
- **Solo is possible at some hosts, not all.** Program level: the IBB FAQ (Nov 2022) says sole founders can be funded; teams are "explicitly desired". No guideline text excluding solo founders was found. The 2-person minimum is a **host-level** rule.
- **Solo-accepting hosts:** **Startup Incubator Berlin (SIB, HWR)** says so on its own page (funded minimum: "one person for a solo application"). An unofficial 2026 guide (stipendium.berlin) also lists **Avant Now**, **MotionLab.Berlin** and **Grace** — Grace is women-only, Avant Now markets to first-time female/FLINTA founders in deep tech / vertical AI / impact tech (check whether men are formally excluded), MotionLab is hardware-oriented. BHT's page says "1–4 founders per team" but its status as a current host is unclear.
- **Teams-only hosts:** Science & Startups (TU + HU + FU + Charité): "Teams of 2–4 … Solo founders are ineligible." HTW Berlin: "mindestens zwei, maximal vier Teammitglieder". So TU's "no solo founders" is true for TU's own channel, not for BSS as a whole.
- **Money:** up to **€2,500 per person per month** (gross), non-repayable, no equity, max 12 months (hosts run 6–12; SIB 6 months extendable to 12; HTW ≥8 + optional 4). Raised from €2,200 in the 2nd call. Funded 40 % ESF+, 60 % Land Berlin.
- **Hard rules found:** main residence in Berlin, implementation in Berlin, full-time commitment, not yet incorporated (S&S also: "none of the team members may have founded a startup before"), no parallel EXIST or second BSS. Nothing found on revenue/side income/BAföG — ask each host and the BAföG office.
- **Degree:** "Zugelassen werden auch Personen ohne Hochschulabschluss, vorausgesetzt sie verfügen selbst über technisches Fachwissen …" — so Bachelor + prototype is fine; no rule on enrolment or degree age found. HTW asks for a completed degree, prototype/MVP, ≥150 h/month, Berlin registration.

## Statistics found
- S&S 1st call (Jul 2023–Nov 2025): 38 teams, 137 scholarship holders (~3.6 per team).
- 2nd call: up to €31 m total; ≥€6.6 m earmarked for "BSS Women" (teams ≥51 % women). "680" funded founders (or teams — sources differ; €31 m/680 ≈ €45.6k only makes sense per person).
- Host budgets: S&S €8 m, HWR ~€3.2 m, HTW ~€2.5 m → rough implied capacity ~175 / ~70 / ~55 persons over the call (own estimate).
- No applicant-to-funded ratios published. TU's verbal figure (80 applicants → 15–20 pitches → ~1/3 win) implies ~7 % overall, ~33 % once you pitch.

## Risks and timing
- The ESF+ guideline v2.3 (3 Jul 2026) reportedly lapses 30 Mar 2027 (same clause existed in v1.8, so probably routine), no 3rd call announced. Plan for cohorts starting by mid-2027.
- Gap from deadline to start is ~7–9 weeks at every host.
- "April batch" mentioned by TU matches nothing published; S&S and HTW both show **March 2027**.

## Science & Startups extras
- Pre-Incubation Club: roughly monthly evenings, one topic each, bring your own idea, panel + "get-together with possible co-founders"; explicitly a preparation step toward BSS, EXIST and the **AI NATION Grant** (new program listed at https://science-startups.berlin/programs/ai-nation-grant — unverified content, worth asking about on 12 Nov).
- Co-founder matching runs on **Match'em** (https://matchem.science-startups.berlin/) — 150+ listings incl. "technical co-founder wanted" posts tied to BSS/EXIST applications.
- Other pages: EXIST Startup Grant, Academy, free support for founders & talents.
