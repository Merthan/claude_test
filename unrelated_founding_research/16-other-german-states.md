# Founder stipends in other German states (only relevant if you would relocate)

Status 31 Oct 2026. Berlin is covered in `10-berlin-startup-stipendium.md`. Research on this topic was cut short by the search budget, so the table mixes **verified-by-search** rows with rows marked *(memory — verify)*, which come from background knowledge and were not checked this session.

**Key point for a Berlin resident:** almost every state program tests your **personal main residence** (Hauptwohnsitz) in that state, not just a company seat. Commuting from Berlin to Potsdam does not satisfy that. Only Hamburg's InnoFounder appears to test the company seat instead (unverified).

## What this means for you
- **None of these beats Berlin for a solo founder** once you know that Startup Incubator Berlin (HWR) runs a solo-friendly BSS track at €2,500/month and that EXIST is solo-friendly nationwide (see `11-exist.md`).
- If you ever did relocate, **NRW** (solo-explicit, €1,200, many networks) and **Hamburg** (highest amount, company-seat test) are the two to look at. Both are a worse deal than EXIST's €2,500 graduate rate.
- **Brandenburg is not a shortcut**: EXIST via Uni Potsdam/HPI would be the same federal program you can also access from Berlin; ILB grants need a Brandenburg company.
