# Berlin ecosystem beyond BSS and EXIST — grants, competitions, incubators, co-founder matching

Status 31 Oct 2026. Facts come from search summaries citing official pages (ibb-business-team.de, b-p-w.de, visionhealthpioneers.de were blocked in the sandbox). Tags: [P] official page as quoted by search; [S] third-party; [M] background knowledge, unverified.

## Top 5 for a solo software founder in the next 12 months
1. **BPW — Businessplan-Wettbewerb Berlin-Brandenburg 2027** [P]: free, idea stage OK, solo OK. **Mandatory "Call to Start" Wed 4 or Thu 5 Nov 2026** (online, 10:00–16:00, 5-minute talk on idea and motivation), then **Phase 1 deadline Tue 10 Nov 2026, 18:00**; Phase 2 **Tue 16 Feb 2027**; Phase 3 **Tue 27 Apr 2027**. Prizes €10k / €15k / €20k per phase plus audience prizes (€1k/2k/3k); every plan read by two independent jurors with written feedback; free academy (seminars, workshops). Entry possible in any phase; plan in German or English. Requirement: founding in Berlin/Brandenburg planned or ≤12 months old. The 2026 handbook has a "Gründungsteam" chapter — ask how a solo founder fills it (advisors, domain experts). https://www.b-p-w.de/en/competition/ · dates https://www.b-p-w.de/en/dates/
2. **Science & Startups (TU/HU/FU/Charité)** [P]: Pre-Incubation Club Thu 12 Nov 2026 18:00–20:00, Salzufer 15/16 (free, no application, open to all); **Match'em** co-founder/role platform incl. Charité (https://matchem.science-startups.berlin/en/); **AI NATION Grant** for pre-incorporation AI teams — next round reportedly ~Dec 2026, start Mar 2027 (unconfirmed; the sibling AI NATION Accelerator page shows a 4 Nov 2026 deadline) → ask on 12 Nov whether ShadowAI/AnyPresent fit and whether solo is possible. https://science-startups.berlin/programs/ai-nation-grant · https://www.science-startups.berlin/for-founders-talents
3. **IBB GründungsBONUS Plus (GBP)** [P/S]: non-repayable grant, **50 % of eligible costs, max €50,000 over up to 2 years**; eligible costs include founder salary up to €2,000 gross/month/person, personnel, external services, IT/IP, running costs; paid in up to 4 instalments. Requires a **Berlin-seated company ≤18 months old** at application (one guide says 12 — confirm), <10 employees, founder holds >50 %; innovative or sustainable model (high-tech not required); seat stays in Berlin ≥3 years; **no spending before the application arrives**. Rolling; guideline dated 16 Jan 2026; applications until **30 Sep 2027**. Solo/freelancers reportedly OK. The other 50 % must be own funds — this is where a €10k mentor contribution or AnyPresent revenue could serve as co-financing. Sequence: found entity → apply → then spend. Ask IBB whether a new UG for AnyPresent counts as a fresh founding if your 2021 app income runs through an older Gewerbe, and about cumulation with BSS/EXIST. Guideline PDF https://www.ibb-business-team.de/fileadmin/ibb-business-team/gruendungsbonus/downloads/foerderrichtlinie-gruendungsbonus-plus.pdf · https://www.ibb-business-team.de/gruendungsbonus-plus/antragstellung-rechtliches/
4. **Vision Health Pioneers (VHP)** [P]: Berlin health-tech incubator for first-time founders (Startup Colors UG; ESF+/Land Berlin; it is a BSS carrier, so the money is BSS money: **€2,500/month per person, 10 months, equity-free**, teams up to 3). Teams of 2–3 with at least one member with healthcare knowledge (physician, nurse, therapist…) and/or active support from healthcare professionals; **solo applicants: "we can help you find a partner"**. Berlin resident for the whole programme; VHP must be your only full-time commitment. **Batch 2 applications 15 Jan – 28 Feb 2027, start 1 Apr 2027** (Senate overview 20 Apr 2026); Batch 3 applications 1–31 Jan 2028. Natural fit for "you + the doctor you know" (doctor tools) or "+ psychologist". Excludes applicants already holding EXIST/BSS. https://visionhealthpioneers.de/application/ · https://visionhealthpioneers.de/faq/
5. **Co-founder / domain-expert search**: **SIBB Startup Co-Founder Matching Thu 19 Nov 2026, 18:00–21:00, CIC Berlin** (curated matching, pre-event questionnaire, chance to join the SIBB Incubator; previous edition 15 Apr 2026 → roughly twice a year). https://www.eventbrite.de/e/startup-co-founder-matching-tickets-2003907663766 · HWR SIB Demo Day Thu 5 Nov 2026, CIC Berlin (networking) · VHP hackathon/team formation (TAIKAI) · Meetup co-founder groups · YC Co-Founder Matching (free, online) [M].

Skip: Pro FIT Frühphase (50 % grant bundled with a 50 % interest-free loan → conflicts with no-loans), Coaching BONUS (ended), Digitalprämie (closed 2023), InvestitionsBONUS (closed Nov 2024), Transfer BONUS (needs an existing SME; guideline ends 31 Dec 2026). Loans (Berlin Start, Mikrokredit, Berlin Innovativ): not recommended by your criteria.

## University offers open to externals (beyond BSS)
- **BHT Startup Hub**: coaching, free co-working (Residenzstraße 37), mentoring for students, staff **and founding-interested Berlin residents**; Tuesday open consultation 13:00–16:00; gruenden@bht-berlin.de. https://www.bht-berlin.de/startup
- **HWR Startup Incubator Berlin (CIC)**: coaching, workspace, prototyping/user testing; "Startup Now" pre-development track (terms not found); ESF+ "Startup Now for Impact" vibe-coding sprints for social/ecological founders starting 2027 (staff being hired). https://www.startup-incubator.berlin/
- **HTW Gründungsservice**: Startup Tuesday workshops, Sommeruni, Thursday open office (likely HTW members; ESF project ended Sep 2026, continuation unconfirmed). https://entrepreneurship.htw-berlin.de/
- **Humboldt-Innovation / FU Profund**: affiliation-based (HU/FU members); FU Startup Villa; HU thematic programmes (K.I.E.Z. AI → likely succeeded by AI NATION). Not your door.
- **TU Centre for Entrepreneurship**: "StarTUp" incubation process and workshops (dates not retrieved). https://www.tu.berlin/entrepreneurship/

## Antler Berlin ("Antler ONE") [S only]
~10-week residency then investment decision; applications year-round; cohorts March and September (next **March 2027**); terms reported as up to €500k pre-seed incl. €200k upfront for ~10 % (unconfirmed, varies by source); no fee, no stipend confirmed for Berlin; solo entry OK (ideas/prototypes/MVP, no revenue needed) but funding goes to teams formed in the residency; full-time in person; < 1 % accepted. Fit: medium fallback if the solo routes fail. https://www.antler.co/continental-europe
