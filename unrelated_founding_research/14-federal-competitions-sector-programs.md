# German nationwide competitions, prizes and sector programs — detail file

Status 31 Oct 2026. Facts come from search summaries of official pages (sites were blocked in the sandbox). Tags: [conf] stated on a primary page per search; [pattern] inferred from last season; [bg] background knowledge, unverified.

## Headlines
1. **Prototype Fund, Cohort 03 — the best odds × money for you.** [conf] €47,500 for 6 months as an individual (team of up to 4: €95,000), plus an optional "Second Stage" (~€31,667) → ~€79,000 over 10 months. Funded period Jun–Nov 2027 (Second Stage Dec 2027–Mar 2028). Open-source software only; individuals 18+ living in Germany; companies/universities cannot apply. Lines: **Innovation** (data security, software infrastructure), **Resilienz** ("freie Alternativen zu proprietärer, zentralisierter und dominierender Software"), **Up and Coming** (same fields, "für alle unter 26 oder in einem Studium oder in einer informatischen Ausbildung" → you qualify via the Master enrolment; confirm wording). **Deadline Mon 30 Nov 2026, 15:00 CET.** Decision expected ~Feb/Mar 2027. Full detail in `12-open-source-funding.md`.
2. **Gründungswettbewerb – Digitale Innovationen (BMWE) is suspended.** [conf] BMWE page (24.03.2026): "Der Gründungswettbewerb wird derzeit nicht fortgeführt." Förderdatenbank: "Antragstellung derzeit nicht möglich". Listings showing 2026 rounds are outdated. It used to be the best solo-friendly prize (€32k/€7k, ~8–9 % odds, natural persons in Germany). Watch for a relaunch; nothing announced.
3. **DBU Green Start-up** — [conf] grant up to **€125,000 over 24 months**, 100 % funding, de minimis, incl. **up to €2,000/month per person**; rolling, no deadline; company (UG/GmbH, ≤5 years old) must exist "spätestens zu Beginn des Förderzeitraums"; the DBU sets no team-size rule ("Feste Vorgaben … macht die DBU nicht", recommends 2+). Software qualifies only with measurable environmental relief (DBU funded "Software-as-a-Service für … Circular Economy"). ~13 start-ups funded per year. Fit: only **Itemz** framed as longer product life / repair / resale. Not AnyPresent or ShadowAI.
4. **Science4Life Venture Cup 2026/27** — [conf] free coaching (Academy Days), prizes €25k/€10k/€5k; entry possible in any phase. 2026/27 dates not published; [pattern] Konzeptphase ~mid-Jan 2027, Businessplanphase ~mid-Apr 2027 (2026: 12 Jan / 13 Apr). Life sciences & chemistry (digital health usually counts [bg] — confirm). Fit: **doctor tools** or mental health only.
5. **Deutschlandstipendium (TU Berlin)** — [conf] €300/month for ≥2 semesters, **not offset against BAföG**; window 15 Jun–15 Jul (2027 [pattern]); requires enrolment at WS start with ≥2 semesters left in Regelstudienzeit. Criteria: performance + social engagement + personal circumstances. Bridge money, not founder money.
6. **Cheap lottery tickets:** Microsoft Imagine Cup 2027 (solo OK; deadline 28 Feb 2027 23:59 UTC per FAQ or 2 Mar per apply page; Student Startup tier capped at a "Student Prize" + $5k Azure); Red Bull Basement DE (individual or duo, 18+, world final up to $100k; 2026/27 not announced, [pattern] Q1–Q2 2027).
7. **Kultur- und Kreativpiloten Deutschland** — title + 1-year mentoring, no cash, solo fine, software/games count as creative sub-market [bg]; past deadlines mid-year; no 2025/26 call found.
8. **DiGA is not a solo route.** [conf] 9 Sep 2026 stats: 254 applications → 62 listed, 131 withdrawn, 29 rejected. Needs CE-marked medical device (Class IIa typical), BSI TR-03161, ISO 13485, study budget (est. €0.5–1.5 M). Build mental-health as self-pay wellness or B2B instead.
9. **StartUpSecure (BMFTR IT-security incubators)** — alive (Demo Days Jun 2026) but research-spin-off format via ATHENE/CISPA/KASTEL/Cube 5; no 2026 call, amounts or solo rule found. ShadowAI only fits as security/privacy research attached to an incubator. Low priority.
10. **Gründungszuschuss / Einstiegsgeld** — not applicable (needs ALG I claim / Bürgergeld; BAföG-eligible students excluded) [bg].
