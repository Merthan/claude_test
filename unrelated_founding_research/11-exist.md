# EXIST-Gründungsstipendium — detail file

Status 31 Oct 2026. Sources are search summaries of the cited official documents (exist.de itself was blocked in the sandbox). Quotes are as relayed by the search tool.

## The short version
- **Solo is allowed by federal rule.** The Richtlinie budgets Sachausgaben "bis zu 10 000 Euro für Einzelgründungsprojekte bzw. bis zu 30 000 Euro für Teamgründungsprojekte (bis zu drei Personen)". The 2026 Handbuch (7th ed.): "1 bis max. 3 Stipendiatinnen/Stipendiaten möglich". TU's "no single applicants" is a **host** decision (the university is the legal applicant and can refuse), not an EXIST rule. Your notes record it only as a recollection — re-ask TU in writing, and ask other hosts.
- **"Research-based" is half true.** No research results are needed (that is EXIST-Forschungstransfer). The stipend funds "Entwicklung marktfähiger Produkte und Dienstleistungen, die Ausreifung einer Geschäftsidee zu einem Businessplan sowie die gezielte Vorbereitung einer Unternehmensgründung". What makes it academic: a **mentor who is a professor or research-group leader at the applying institution**, and the host's Gründungsnetzwerk must confirm it advised you.
- **Open now.** Rolling applications, any time; applications accepted until **31 Dec 2027**; guideline valid to 31 Dec 2029. No Antragsstopp or budget freeze found for 2025–26 (Handbuch refreshed Aug 2026, forms dated Sep/Oct 2025, BMWE one-pager Nov 2025). Confirm with Projektträger Jülich (PtJ).
- **Money (12 months):** Absolvent:innen €2,500/month; Studierende €1,000/month; Promovierte €3,000; Berufsausbildung €2,000; +€150 per child; Sachausgaben €10,000 (solo) / €30,000 (team); Coaching €5,000. Solo graduate package ≈ €45,000; solo student package ≈ €27,000.

## The catch that matters most for you: student vs graduate status
- Ministry Q&A (existenzgruendungsportal, BMWK-Infopool, 2021–2025): "Als Studierender im Masterstudium mit erfolgreich abgeschlossenem Bachelorstudium zählen Sie als Studierender …" → **€1,000 rate while enrolled**, even in a Urlaubssemester ("Sie gelten weiterhin als eingeschriebener Student").
- Exmatriculation needed for the graduate rate? Ministry answer: "Diese Frage kann ich kurz mit „ja“ beantworten."
- Worse: a solo applicant who is a student is a team "mehrheitlich Studierende", which is "grundsätzlich nicht förderfähig" (2026 Handbuch: only "in begründeten Ausnahmefällen").
- Consequence: **for solo EXIST you most likely have to exmatriculate** (→ BAföG ends, student health insurance ends) and apply as a graduate (Bachelor 2025/26 is well inside the 5-year window). Whether status is judged at application, approval or start is **not verified** — ask PtJ in writing before you de-register.
- Alternative: add one graduate co-founder → team no longer "mostly students", Sachausgaben €30k. Against your preference, but it is the only way to keep enrolment.

| Scenario | Rate | Package | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stay enrolled, solo | €1,000 | ≈ €27k | "mostly students" rule → likely not fundable; BAföG conflict |
| Exmatriculate, solo | €2,500 | ≈ €45k | timing of status check unknown; BAföG + student KV end |
| Add a graduate co-founder | €2,500 each | ≈ €75k+ | not your preference |

## Other rules found
- Parallel funding: "Eine zeitgleiche Kombination mit einem anderen Stipendium, aktiven Beschäftigungsverhältnis oder einem Förderprogramm zur Finanzierung des Lebensunterhalts … ist ausgeschlossen. Ein ruhendes Beschäftigungsverhältnis (Gründungssabbatical) ist zulässig." → no BAföG, no BSS, no Prototype Fund at the same time.
- Degree recency: "generally for up to five years after graduation".
- Students must have completed ≥ half of their Studienleistungen (waived when a Master builds on a finished Bachelor).
- Host = any German Hochschule/Forschungseinrichtung with a Gründungsnetzwerk; it signs the Stipendiatenverträge and files with PtJ **before** the project starts. No rule found that it must be *your* university — but no confirmation that externals are accepted either. Ask.
- Existing business: the exact "noch nicht gegründet" wording and its treatment of a small existing Gewerbe/app income was **not verified**. Disclose Bluetooth Presenter openly; the venture you apply with must not be founded yet.
- Tax: guides call the EXIST stipend taxable (Anlage SO; reserve 15–30 %); no source applies § 3 Nr. 44 EStG to it — do not plan on tax-free. Side-job hour limits not verified. See `17-rules-bafoeg-taxes-legal.md`.

## Practical path (inference from the rules)
1. Email PtJ (ptj-exist-gruendungsstipendium@fz-juelich.de, +49 30 20199-411 — verify) with the written questions below.
2. Line up a **professor-mentor** at the host you want to apply through (Bachelor thesis supervisor, or a professor in speech/HCI/software at TU; or a professor at HTW/BHT/HWR if they host you).
3. Ask 2–3 Gründungsnetzwerke the same questions in writing: Science & Startups (use 12 Nov), one Fachhochschule (HTW or BHT), one more (HWR Startup Incubator, Uni Potsdam).
4. Exmatriculate only after PtJ confirms when status is assessed.
5. Prepare the host's pre-selection paper (Ideenpapier): innovation + market + why you alone can build it (Bluetooth Presenter traction is your evidence).

## Questions to put in writing
**To PtJ:** (1) Is the Studierende/Absolvent rate fixed at application, approval or Förderbeginn? Must an enrolled Master student with a finished Bachelor exmatriculate for €2,500? (2) Is an enrolled solo applicant a "mehrheitlich Studierende" team? (3) May a TU Berlin graduate apply through another Hochschule? Any link to it required? (4) Side jobs/hour limits; BAföG explicit rule. (5) Does a privately published Play Store app with revenue since 2021 count as an already started business? (6) Current processing time and approval rate; budget intact?
**To each host:** solo accepted? externals accepted? mentor matching? pre-selection format and deadlines? is "no solo" an EXIST policy or only BSS?
