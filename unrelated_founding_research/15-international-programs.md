# International programs for a solo founder based in Berlin — detail file

Status 31 Oct 2026. Only search summaries were available (pages themselves were blocked), so treat every date as "check the official page before you plan around it". Tags: [O] stated on an official page as relayed by search; [T] third-party only; [M] background knowledge, unchecked.

## Ranking for you (acceptance realism × money × compatibility with staying mostly in Berlin)
1. **Y Combinator W27** — deadline **Mon 2 Nov 2026, 8 pm PT (Tue 3 Nov 05:00 Berlin)** [O]. $500K ($125K for 7 % + $375K uncapped MFN SAFE), Jan–Mar 2027 in San Francisco, "regularly accepts solo founders" [O]. One evening of work; YC also lets you tick "a batch after Winter 2027" (Early Decision) if you prefer Spring/Summer 2027. Odds ~1–2 % [M]. Costs nothing to try.
2. **Entrepreneur First "The Bridge", Winter 27** — SF, 8 weeks, start Feb 2027, **deadline 23 Nov 2026** [O]. No co-founder or idea needed; housing + board + visa support [O]; path to $125K for 8 % (+ optional $125K). Rule to check: "for young Europeans who graduated in 2023 or later" [O, ~13 months old]. EF's purpose is co-founder matching, so this is the "compromise" path, but short and fully funded.
3. **Techstars London, Spring 2027** — **deadline 18 Nov 2026** [O]; 8 Mar–3 Jun 2027; $220K ($20K for 5 % + $200K MFN SAFE). Solo stance unclear (sources conflict), UK work right needed (Germans have none automatically) [M].
4. **South Park Commons Founder Fellowship, Spring 2027** — applications "later this year" [O]; expect a deadline around **1 Feb 2027** (Spring 2026 pattern). $400K for 7 % + $600K guaranteed at next round; "solo founders are welcome … you don't even need your final startup idea yet" [T quoting SPC]; 8-week bootcamp in SF/NYC/Bengaluru, then open-ended residency.
5. **Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs (EYE)** — rolling, equity-free; 1–6 months with a host entrepreneur in another EU country; monthly allowance by host country (Germany-based new entrepreneurs: ~€900–1,100 depending on destination) [O/T]. Berlin intermediaries listed: ABUplus International GmbH, STIC Wirtschaftsfördergesellschaft [O, Aug 2026]. Risk: "new entrepreneur" means < 3 years running a business — your app income since 2021 may count if it runs through a registered business. It funds learning abroad, not building AnyPresent.
6. **Emergent Ventures (Mercatus Center)** — rolling, remote, equity-free; 1,500-word form, "applications encouraged from all ages and all parts of the world", AI-written applications rejected [O]; no published grant range (third parties: $10K–$100K, often ~$50K) [T]. A cheap lottery ticket for a talent bet: pitch "26-year-old who shipped a 50k-download app and won 5 hackathons".

Next tier: **a16z speedrun** ($500K for 10 % + $500K follow-on; solo allowed but "generally prefer teams"; 12 weeks SF; one page says the SR008 window closes **1 Nov 2026** — unverified; next window ~May 2027) · **Solo Founders Program** (Julian Weisser; $100K, 3 months SF, solo-only, 10 spots; terms/dates unverified — the most on-point program by name, verify first) · **EF London Spring 2027** (Apr start; £6,000 equity-free "talent investment" + up to $250K; applications not open yet) · **Antler London** (rolling; £210K day zero; UK work right required) · **Antler ONE Berlin** (residencies March & September, applications all year, "up to €500K", no stipend, < 1 % accepted [O]) · **Start-Up Chile** (equity-free CLP 15M "Build" ≈ €14K for 4 months in Chile; BIG 13 call plausibly Nov 2026–Jan 2027; 50 % of Build places reserved for women-led projects).

Not for you: Thiel Fellowship (age ≤ 22), Pioneer (closed 2024), Neo Accelerator (replaced by Neo Residency; dates unknown), Alpha Fellowship (US work authorisation), Women TechEU (women only), EIC (companies/consortia), Canada/PT/ES/IT/EE startup visas (no money, irrelevant for an EU citizen).
