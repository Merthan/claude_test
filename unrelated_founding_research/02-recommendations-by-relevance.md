# Recommendations sorted by relevance for you

Ranking logic: realistic chance **as a solo founder** × money × fit with "Berlin, no loans, done within 12 months". The same items appear by date in `01-recommendations-by-date.md`. Odds are published figures where they exist, otherwise my judgement.

## Tier 1 — do these
1. **Prototype Fund, Class 03** — deadline Mon 30 Nov 2026, 15:00 CET. €47,500 for 6 months as an individual (≈ €7.9k gross/month) + optional €31,667 second stage; funding 1 Jun 2027. Historic odds ~8 %. Requires open source and a fit with "data security / software infrastructure" (Innovation, Up and Coming) or "free alternatives to proprietary, centralised software" (Resilienz). Best candidates: the on-device speech engine as a reusable library (Resilienz), an open core of ShadowAI (Innovation), CaptivePortalAutoLogin (Innovation, small). Watch the student rule (max 20 h/week for enrolled students) — ask the office hour whether a zero-module student counts; otherwise exmatriculate before 1 Jun 2027. Detail: `12-open-source-funding.md`.
2. **EXIST-Gründungsstipendium** — rolling until 31 Dec 2027. Solo is explicitly allowed ("Einzelgründungsprojekte", €10k Sachausgaben). As a **graduate** €2,500/month × 12 + €10k + €5k coaching (~€45k); as an enrolled student only €1,000 and a solo student counts as a "mostly students" team, which is normally not fundable → plan to **exmatriculate** (BAföG ends with the stipend anyway). Needs a professor mentor and a host university with a Gründungsnetzwerk; TU's "no solo" is TU's choice, so ask HTW, BHT, HWR, Uni Potsdam too. Realistic timeline: prepare Dec–Feb, submit ~Mar 2027, start ~Jun–Aug 2027. Detail: `11-exist.md`.
3. **Berliner Startup Stipendium via Startup Incubator Berlin (HWR)** — the one Berlin BSS host that states it funds solo founders ("one person for a solo application"). €2,500/month gross, 6 months extendable to 12, equity-free. BSS VI just started (deadline was 13 Sep 2026); BSS VII dates are not published — email them this week and go to their Demo Day on 5 Nov. Watch: no company founded, no EXIST in parallel, Berlin residence, full time. Detail: `10-berlin-startup-stipendium.md`.
4. **Vision Health Pioneers, Batch 2** (apply 15 Jan–28 Feb 2027, start 1 Apr 2027) — €2,500/month for 10 months, equity-free, team of 2–3 with a healthcare professional. With your urologist or psychologist contact this is the one program that matches your ideal team (domain expert, no business co-founder) and it starts before your money deadline. VHP helps solo applicants find a partner if yours drops out. Detail: `13-berlin-ecosystem-grants-accelerators.md`.
5. **BPW Businessplan-Wettbewerb Berlin-Brandenburg 2027** — free, solo OK, idea stage OK. Call to Start 4/5 Nov, Phase 1 by 10 Nov, Phase 2 by 16 Feb, Phase 3 by 27 Apr 2027. Prizes €10k/15k/20k per phase, written feedback from two jurors every round, free seminars. This is the "coaching without money" program you asked for, and the feedback doubles as EXIST/BSS preparation. Detail: `13-berlin-ecosystem-grants-accelerators.md`.
6. **Science & Startups: Pre-Incubation Club (12 Nov) + AI NATION Grant + Match'em** — free, no application. Use 12 Nov to (a) get the EXIST host/mentor questions answered, (b) find out what the AI NATION Grant for pre-incorporation AI teams is (reportedly next round ~Dec 2026, start Mar 2027) and whether solo works, (c) look at Match'em for a domain-expert partner if you ever need one.

## Tier 2 — strong, but each has a condition
7. **Entrepreneur First "The Bridge" Winter 27** (deadline 23 Nov; SF, Feb 2027, 8 weeks, housing + board + visa paid) — you are eligible (degree Oct 2026) and you said forced matching is fine. The cheapest way to try an elite program without giving up Berlin: no obligation if no company comes out of it.
8. **IBB GründungsBONUS Plus** — 50 % of eligible costs, max €50k over 2 years, non-repayable, founder salary up to €2,000/month eligible; rolling until 30 Sep 2027. Condition: a Berlin UG/GmbH ≤18 months old and no spending before applying. Good match for the moment AnyPresent becomes a company; the mentor's €10k can be part of the 50 % own share. Not combinable with BSS/EXIST for the same idea (they require "not founded").
9. **HTW BSS Batch 2** (23 Nov–10 Jan, start 1 Mar 2027) and **S&S March 2027 batch** — €2,500/month but teams of 2–4 only. Condition: you accept one second person (domain expert at ~20 % will not pass; hosts want committed members). Use SIBB Co-Founder Matching (19 Nov) or Match'em.
10. **Y Combinator** (W27 by 2 Nov, or a later 2027 batch) — solo accepted, $500k, but ~1–2 % odds and 3 months in SF. Cheap to apply; AnyPresent's Bluetooth Presenter traction is a real asset in the form. Related long shots: **South Park Commons** (~1 Feb 2027, $400k, "solo founders welcome"), **Emergent Ventures** (rolling, remote, 1,500 words), **a16z speedrun** (~May 2027).
11. **NLnet Restack** (3 Nov 2026, then ~3 Jan 2027) — €5k–50k, individuals OK. Condition: only CaptivePortalAutoLogin framed as open-internet-stack work; AI is out of scope.

## Explicitly not recommended
Loans of any kind (Pro FIT early phase bundles a loan, IBB Berlin Start, Mikrokredit) · Gründungswettbewerb Digitale Innovationen (suspended) · Grace / women-only tracks · DiGA fast-track as a solo developer · Thiel, Hult, Samsung (age/team rules) · relocating for another state's stipend (NRW €1,200 and Hamburg's InnoFounder are the only solo-friendly ones worth knowing; all require living there).
