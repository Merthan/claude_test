# Questions for you (answer while the research runs)

Short answers are fine. "Unsure" is a valid answer. Numbers refer to what each answer unlocks.

## A. Hard eligibility facts
1. **When exactly did you finish the Bachelor** (month/year)? EXIST pays the graduate rate (€2,500/month vs €1,000 for students) and has a "within 5 years of degree" window.
2. **When does BAföG end** (month/year), and is it Master BAföG? Would you drop BAföG for a stipend that pays more but requires full-time commitment (BSS, EXIST)?
3. **Citizenship**: German passport? Turkish passport too? (Affects US visas for YC/SPC-type programs, and whether Turkish programs like TÜBİTAK BİGG are even an option.)
4. **Bluetooth Presenter**: is it run as a registered Gewerbe / Kleinunternehmer / freelance, or privately via the Play store? Since when, and roughly what yearly revenue? This matters for: EXIST and BSS "not yet founded" rules, and Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs' "fewer than 3 years as an entrepreneur" rule.
5. **Enrollment**: will you stay enrolled in the Master for the whole next year (even with zero modules)? Some programs want "student", some want "graduate", some forbid full-time studies.

## B. AnyPresent and the "grey zone"
6. AnyPresent launches in ~1 month. **Paid from day one** (subscription / one-off), or free first? Under which entity?
7. Would you **delay monetization or keep AnyPresent free** until a stipend decision, if that keeps it eligible for BSS/EXIST? Or would you rather **apply with a different idea** and keep AnyPresent as the independent side business?
8. Is AnyPresent's speech engine something you would **open source** (fully or partly) if that unlocked €50k–€95k of open-source funding (Prototype Fund, NLnet)?

## C. Which idea goes into applications
9. **Rank the ideas** you would realistically put forward in the next 3 months: AnyPresent / speech coach, Itemz, ShadowAI, Doctor tools, Senior phone tool, CaptivePortalAutoLogin, Anti-ADHD app, Mental-health DiGA, other.
10. Which of these are you willing to build **fully open source** (a hard requirement for Prototype Fund, NLnet, Media Tech Lab)? Senior phone tool and CaptivePortalAutoLogin you already marked as possible.
11. **Doctor tools**: would the doctor you know agree to be named as an advisor on an application, and roughly which specialty? Any psychologist contacts?
12. **Green tech**: any concrete software idea, or just "open to it"? (Decides whether DBU Green Start-up / ClimateLaunchpad are worth your time.)

## D. Team, equity, relocation
13. For a team-only program (BSS): would you accept a **domain-expert co-founder at ~20% time** (doctor/psychologist), a **second developer**, or neither?
14. Would you accept **forced co-founder matching** (Antler, Entrepreneur First style: you join alone, must form a team in weeks)?
15. **Equity instead of a loan**: OK with giving e.g. 7–10% for €100k–€450k (YC, Antler, EF)? Or do you only want non-dilutive money right now?
16. **Relocation for a paid program**: (a) 3–6 months abroad (London/Paris/SF/Seoul/Santiago)? (b) another German state (NRW, Hamburg, Saxony, Brandenburg) where solo stipends exist but you must live/found there? (c) Berlin only?
17. Would you found a **UG/GmbH in Berlin within the next 6 months** if that unlocked a company grant (IBB GründungsBONUS, up to €50k) even though it ends BSS/EXIST eligibility for that idea?

## E. Money and time
18. **Minimum monthly amount** you need to live in Berlin (so I can judge whether €1,000–1,200 stipends are viable vs €2,200–2,500)?
19. **Latest acceptable start** for a stipend (BSS batches start Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct; EXIST is rolling with ~3–4 months processing)?
20. How much time per week can you give to **programs without money** (coaching, pre-incubation, business plan competitions) until a paid one starts?

## F. Background details that sharpen the pitch
21. Which **5 hackathons did you win**, and the topics (health? AI? accessibility?)? This decides which "track record" we lead with per program.
22. Have you **applied anywhere before** (EXIST, BSS, Prototype Fund, Antler, accelerators)? Outcomes?
23. Are you already **in contact** with any Berlin incubator besides TU (HTW, BHT, HWR Startup Incubator, Charité/BIH, Vision Health Pioneers, Humboldt-Innovation, FU Profund)?
24. Would you consider a **non-founding stipend** as bridge money (e.g., Deutschlandstipendium €300/month at TU, Google Summer of Code, LFX Mentorship) while preparing the real application?
