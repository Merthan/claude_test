# Stipend clarifications: before versus during, BAföG, and sequencing

## The misunderstanding, resolved

Both programmes draw a line at the start date of the funding:

- **Before the start:** the company must not be founded, and the venture's business activity must not have started.
- **After the start:** founding a UG or GmbH is normal and expected, usually in the second half of the year. Revenue is allowed and stays in the company. The stipend is your personal living allowance. What is restricted during the funding: a job above five hours a week, another living-cost stipend, and (as I understand it; verify) drawing a salary from the company while you receive the stipend.

The answer you got from another model was about "during". My concern was about "before". Both are right.

## What this means for your plan

- A free launch phase in December or January, with no revenue and no company, fits "before" under any likely reading. Confirm it in one sentence with the founder service.
- The old Pro app still sells a little every month. Unpublish it when the free launch goes live. Owners keep it installed, and the new app grandfathers them. Then nothing is selling in the pre-start window.
- When the funding starts (1 January 2027 for Berlin Batch IV, later for EXIST), Pro gets priced once the Phase 2 features are ready, revenue flows to the venture, and the UG is founded mid-year when revenue or pilots justify it.
- The only scenario that changes the plan: the service says no public launch at all before the start. Then run closed testing as wide as the stores allow (TestFlight up to 10,000 external testers, Play closed testing with large lists), build the whole surface, and launch on day one of the funding.

## BAföG, insurance, student status

- EXIST and Berlin exclude other living-cost stipends, and BAföG ends with exmatriculation anyway.
- BAföG for students is half grant and half interest-free loan, so €1,000 of BAföG costs about €500 of future repayment. Swapping it for a €2,500 tax-free stipend is a larger improvement than the headline difference.
- Health insurance: after exmatriculation the student rate ends; voluntary statutory insurance costs roughly €230 to €300 a month at a low income (check the current figure). Budget it against the stipend.
- Other student perks end (student ticket, discounts). Minor.
- When to exmatriculate: only after the funding is confirmed in writing, and after checking whether EXIST judges the student-or-graduate category at application time or at the start date. Since you hold a Bachelor's, the graduate rate is the likely answer either way, as you say.
- EXIST through your university after exmatriculation remains possible as an alumnus within five years of the degree.

## Berlin versus EXIST at the graduate rate

Both pay about €2,500 a month. EXIST adds up to €10,000 for costs and €5,000 for coaching and carries the federal brand; it starts later (three to six months after submission) and runs twelve months from then. Berlin starts on 1 January if Batch IV accepts you, comes with an incubator programme and its obligations, and ends by December 2027. You cannot take both.

Recommendation: Berlin Batch IV now, if your incubator accepts solo founders and you can live with its programme. EXIST as the fallback, with the launch-phase numbers in the Ideenpapier. If both are possible and the timing is equal, EXIST's extras win.
