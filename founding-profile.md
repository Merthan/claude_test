## Goal
- Wants to found something in Berlin.
- Mainly looking for stipends, or programs to enroll in, that either coach or give money for developing their own venture.
- Has time right now for programs, events and coaching, even without a co-founder. Will definitely attend the Science & Startups Pre-Incubation Club on 12 Nov 2026.
- Would also consider government funding for open-source work (for example as a "main thing" that pays monthly bills), while launching a product such as AnyPresent on the side.

## Working style and team
- Works very well alone and definitely prefers working alone. This was learned from hackathons.
- Would rather start alone and maybe hire working students or find co-founders later.
- The kind of person they'd most like to work with is a real domain expert (medicine, psychology and so on) who doesn't interfere with what they can do themselves and mainly explains the problems. Such an expert isn't necessarily needed as a co-founder; topic consultants or interviews would also work.
- Would really dislike marketing or business co-founders.
- If a program requires a team, would be willing (as a compromise, not a preference) to add a second person with clearly separated, isolated tasks, without lying about it.
- Not bad at selling themselves or an idea, but sees that being a solo founder makes acceptance harder.

## Preferences on funding
- Strongly dislikes loans or credit options. Having to repay a loan after a failure is terrible, whereas with a stipend you only lose time. Not looking for 0-interest loans.
- A mentor willing to put in around €10,000 would be available, but that doesn't make loans attractive.
- Wants options ranked by realistic acceptance chance for a solo founder. A big stipend with tiny acceptance odds, or one that strongly prefers women or large expert teams, shouldn't be option #1.
- Pragmatic, not a researcher: programs that expect PhD-level work or writing papers/white papers aren't realistic. Sees EXIST as rather research-based and one of the weaker options.
- Is not limited to one field. Could found in many areas, including software/AI, medical, psychology and green tech (green tech yes, but nothing like carbon capture that needs a hardware/science side). Some fields might work without experts at the start.

## TU Berlin consultation (notes from a past meeting)
- Berliner Startup Stipendium (BSS): no solo founders.
- EXIST: rather research-based. Recollection: TU doesn't support single applicants for EXIST either.
- TU suggested BSS rather than EXIST. A BSS batch starting in April fits thematically.
- BSS is also possible at other incubators (Senate list, 10–11 incubators).
- If solo: look at IBB, but those are funding programs and loans, "not a real option".
- No UG/GmbH, contracts or payments before a stipend. Revenue is technically not allowed (grey zone).
- BSS odds: of, for example, 80 applicants, 15–20 are invited to pitch, and about a third of those win.

- **Speech training app:** the same engine, refinable over time.
- **Itemz:** a purchase-organizing app that tracks everything in your apartment, shows resources, product discussions and manuals, alerts when warranties run out, and lets you sell things (competing with Kleinanzeigen). Optical recognition and receipt/PDF bill OCR. Not started yet, no proof of concept.
- **CaptivePortalAutoLogin:** a simple utility app; could be open source.
- **Senior phone tool:** makes phones much easier for older people, beyond the OS's built-in features; could be open source.

Seamless data mining while handling the privacy hurdles intelligently.

- **Anti-ADHD phone-usage app and advisor:** a lot of competition already.
- **Mental-health DiGA** (psychology): probably quite hard.
- None of the ideas has a team yet.
