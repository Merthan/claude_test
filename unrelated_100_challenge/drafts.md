# 100-word drafts for "100 to 100"

*Each draft is exactly 100 words by a standard word counter. Re-count in the form's own counter before submitting; counters disagree on hyphens and numbers. Draft 1 is the recommended one. The briefing explains the choices.*

## Draft 1 (recommended): the education shift leads, the coach proves, traction and impact close

Because AI now writes the homework, universities and schools are moving assessment back into the room: more presentations, more oral exams, more talks. Yet presenters still rely on a €10 clicker and get no feedback. AnyPresent turns the phone everyone already owns into the remote, laser and spotlight, a poll screen for the audience, and a private speaking coach: speech is analysed on the device itself, in eight languages, with nothing uploaded, showing where pace, pauses and fillers lose the room. A predecessor reached 46,000 installs; prototypes run on iOS, Android, Windows and macOS. Rehearsal feedback for everyone, without hardware.

## Draft 2: the pocket device leads

Fewer devices, better talks.

## Draft 3: your environmental lead, made defensible

Millions of single-purpose presentation clickers are sold every year and soon thrown away, while a far better device already sits in every presenter's pocket. AnyPresent replaces the clicker with the phone: slides, laser and spotlight with nothing installed on the computer, audience polls, and a private speaking coach whose speech model runs on the phone itself in eight languages, so nothing is uploaded. As AI pushes classrooms back to in-person presentations, students and lecturers get the rehearsal feedback that only paid coaches offered. A predecessor reached 46,000 installs; prototypes run on every major platform. Less hardware, less waste, better speakers.

## Draft 4: the science-forward version (for readers who like one checkable technical claim)

The same app is the presentation remote: slides, laser and spotlight with nothing installed on the computer, plus audience polls. Universities are returning to oral presentations because AI writes the essays; we give every student rehearsal feedback few could afford. A predecessor reached 46,000 installs. Prototypes run on iOS, Android, Windows and macOS.
